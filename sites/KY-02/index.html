The math

How WaterWise works

Three components that connect. A neural network learns the physics. A stochastic layer handles what the neural network can't know. A controller uses both to plan a week ahead.

01

Fourier Neural Operator

Learns to solve the Richards equation from 9 years of NASA SMAP satellite data. Runs in milliseconds instead of hours.

∂θ/∂t = ∇·[K(θ)∇ψ] + S
02

Stochastic Extension

Adds a learned noise function and runs 500 Monte Carlo scenarios, producing calibrated uncertainty bounds for every forecast.

dθ = f dt + σ(x,θ) dW(t)
03

Model Predictive Control

Uses the probabilistic 7-day forecast to minimize water use while keeping soil moisture above the plant wilting point.

P(θ ≥ θ_crit) ≥ 0.95
Testing

Tested in the real world

Two rounds of validation: a hands-on hardware experiment with real plants, and a global simulation across 50 agricultural sites on six continents.

🌱

Hardware Experiment

Tomato plants, Arduino sensors, a 12V pump, and two months of side-by-side comparison between Neural MPC and traditional threshold irrigation.
